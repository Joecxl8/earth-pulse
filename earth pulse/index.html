Orbital MODIS thermal LST suffers from heavy cloud-cover dropouts during Kerala's JJAS monsoon. Never conflate skin temperature with 2 m air temperature.
PRECTOTCORR (POWER) Bias-corrected total rain/snow precipitation mm Merged Reanalysis-Gauge Monthly POWER values are served in mm/day; EARTH PULSE multiplies each month by exact calendar days in that month (including leap years) before summing Annual, JJAS, and MAM totals.
RH2M (POWER) Relative humidity at 2 metres height % Reanalysis Model (MERRA-2) Ratio of actual water vapor pressure to saturation vapor pressure. Crucial for contrasting humid tropical heat stress in Kerala against dry sensible heating in Rajasthan.
MODIS NDVI & ISRO MOSDAC Normalized Difference Vegetation Index & Monsoon Validation Unitless Derived Index & Partner Context Red/NIR reflectance ratio proxy for canopy greenness; contextualized alongside Space Agency Partner ISRO Bhuvan / MOSDAC Indian monsoon regional climatology references.
1. Avoiding Seasonal Autocorrelation Running a naive t-test on 528 raw monthly records is invalid because seasonal monsoon cycles violate serial independence. Slicing into Annual (12-mo), JJAS (4-mo), and MAM (3-mo) series ($n=44$ years) ensures rigorous Mann–Kendall inference.
2. Theil–Sen vs. Outlier Distortion Extreme events (such as the 2018 Kerala floods, flagged with a Rocket Red callout marker on Chart 1) pull ordinary least squares (OLS) regression slopes. Theil–Sen computes the median slope across all $\binom{44}{2} = 946$ year pairs.
3. Significance ≠ Attribution A Mann–Kendall $p\text{-value} < 0.05$ confirms a non-random monotonic trend over 1981–2024. Distinguishing global greenhouse forcing from local irrigation or urbanization requires coupled physical attribution modeling (correlation $\neq$ causation).
